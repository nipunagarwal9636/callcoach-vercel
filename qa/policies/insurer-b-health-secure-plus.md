# Insurer B · Health Secure Plus: Policy Wording (summary)
SYNTHETIC SAMPLE. Fictional insurer and plan, written for the PS-04 Applied AI Challenge demo. Not a real policy.
Product: Health insurance (individual and family floater)

## 1.1 Sum insured
Sum insured options: ₹3 lakh, ₹5 lakh, ₹10 lakh and ₹25 lakh per policy year, as shown in the quote and the policy schedule.

## 2.1 Cashless treatment
Cashless treatment is available only at the insurer's network hospitals. At non-network hospitals the insured pays and claims reimbursement within 30 days of discharge.

## 3.1 Initial waiting period
Initial waiting period: 30 days from the first policy start date. No claim is payable for illness in this period, except accidents.

## 3.2 Specific illness waiting period
Specific illness waiting period: 24 months for listed conditions including cataract, hernia, joint replacement and kidney stones.

## 3.3 Pre-existing disease waiting period
Pre-existing diseases (PED), including diabetes, hypertension and thyroid disorders declared at proposal, are covered after a waiting period of 36 months of continuous cover. A pre-existing disease is not covered from day one.

## 4.1 Room rent
Room rent is limited to 1% of the sum insured per day. If a higher room category is chosen, associated expenses are paid proportionately.

## 4.2 Co-payment
Co-payment: 0% for insured persons aged up to 60 at entry.

## 5.1 Claims
All claims are subject to the policy terms, conditions and exclusions and are decided by the insurer. No intermediary can guarantee approval of a claim.

## 5.2 No return of premium
This plan has no return of premium, no maturity benefit and no payout for a claim-free year.

## 6.1 Free-look period
The policyholder has a free-look period of 30 days from receipt of the policy document to review it and cancel for a refund, less proportionate risk premium and expenses.
