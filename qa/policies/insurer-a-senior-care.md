# Insurer A · Senior Care: Policy Wording (summary)
SYNTHETIC SAMPLE. Fictional insurer and plan, written for the PS-04 Applied AI Challenge demo. Not a real policy.
Product: Health insurance for senior citizens (entry age 60 to 75)

## 1.1 Sum insured
Sum insured options: ₹3 lakh and ₹5 lakh per policy year.

## 2.1 Cashless treatment
Cashless treatment is available only at the insurer's network hospitals. Elsewhere, treatment is paid by the insured and reimbursed.

## 3.1 Initial waiting period
Initial waiting period: 30 days from the policy start date, except accidents.

## 3.3 Pre-existing disease waiting period
Pre-existing diseases declared at proposal are covered after a waiting period of 24 months of continuous cover.

## 4.2 Co-payment
Co-payment: 20% of every admissible claim is borne by the insured.

## 5.1 Claims
All claims are subject to the policy terms, conditions and exclusions and are decided by the insurer. No intermediary can guarantee approval of a claim.

## 5.2 No return of premium
This plan has no return of premium and no payout for a claim-free year.

## 6.1 Free-look period
Free-look period of 30 days from receipt of the policy document.
