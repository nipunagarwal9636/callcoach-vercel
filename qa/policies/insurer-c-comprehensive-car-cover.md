# Insurer C · Comprehensive Car Cover with zero depreciation: Policy Wording (summary)
SYNTHETIC SAMPLE. Fictional insurer and plan, written for the PS-04 Applied AI Challenge demo. Not a real policy.
Product: Motor insurance, private car, comprehensive with zero depreciation add-on

## 1.1 Insured declared value
The insured declared value (IDV) is the value shown in the quote and the policy schedule.

## 2.1 Cashless repairs
Cashless repairs are available only at the insurer's network garages. At other garages the insured pays and claims reimbursement.

## 3.1 Compulsory deductible
A compulsory deductible of ₹1,000 applies to every own-damage claim.

## 3.2 Zero depreciation add-on
Zero depreciation cover is limited to 2 claims per policy year. Further claims are settled with depreciation on parts.

## 3.3 Engine protection
Engine damage caused by water ingress or hydrostatic lock is not covered without the engine protect add-on.

## 5.1 Claims
All claims are subject to the policy terms, conditions and exclusions and are decided by the insurer. No intermediary can guarantee approval of a claim.

## 5.2 No return of premium
This policy has no return of premium.
